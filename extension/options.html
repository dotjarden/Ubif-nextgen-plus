        Updates & help

        Keep the extension in the same folder to retain your settings and column layouts.

        Git: Run npm run update in your checkout, or git pull --ff-only.

        ZIP: Download the newer files and replace the contents of the folder you originally loaded.

        After updating, reload the extension below, then refresh your portal tabs when your work is saved. Unpacked extensions do not update automatically.

        This is a preview. Open settings from Chrome’s extension toolbar to change your installed extension.
